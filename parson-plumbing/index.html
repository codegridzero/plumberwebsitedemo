Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our plumbing services? Find quick, reliable answers below regarding emergency visits, leak repairs, and pricing.
What should I do first in a plumbing emergency?
Locate and shut off your main water valve immediately to halt active water flow and prevent structural damage. Once the valve is closed, call our 24/7 emergency dispatch team to send a certified master plumber straight to your property.
How quickly can your plumbers respond to emergency calls?
For critical emergencies (such as burst water lines, overflowing sewer backups, or major leaks), we guarantee a 30-minute rapid response dispatch across our Ohio service network.
What are the most common signs of a hidden water leak?
Warning signs include unexplained spikes in monthly utility bills, damp or discolored drywall/ceiling spots, musty odors, low water pressure, or hearing running water when all fixtures are turned off.
How can I prevent my drain lines from getting clogged?
Never pour cooking oils, grease, or coffee grounds down kitchen drains, install hair strainers in tub and shower drains, and avoid flushing non-flushable wipes. Periodic hydro-jetting or professional maintenance also keeps main lines clear.
Why is my water heater making a loud knocking or popping noise?
Popping or rumbling noises indicate mineral sediment buildup at the bottom of your heating tank. Annual flushing removes these mineral deposits, restores heating efficiency, and extends the lifespan of your unit.
